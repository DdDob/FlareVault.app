# About Flare Vault

Flare Vault is non-custodial software for Flare Mainnet. You connect your own wallet to see, send and receive FLR, FXRP, bridged USDT and NFTs, and you can optionally set up a Legacy Vault so the people you choose can claim your funds if you stop checking in.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

## Why Flare Vault exists

Digital assets are easy to lose track of. When access to a wallet is lost, or an owner becomes unavailable, family and beneficiaries often have no way to reach what was left behind. Flare Vault makes asset continuity simple on the Flare network: you stay in full control during your lifetime, and you decide who receives your assets, how much each person receives, and how long you can be inactive before they can claim.

## What you can do today

- **Connect your own wallet.** MetaMask, Trust Wallet, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow, or any wallet that supports WalletConnect. There is no account, email or password.
- **See your balances.** FLR, FXRP and bridged USDT on Flare Mainnet, plus your transaction history.
- **Send and receive.** Move FLR and supported tokens to any address. Flare Vault charges no fee for sending; you pay only the network gas.
- **View, receive and send NFTs.** ERC-721 and ERC-1155 NFTs on Flare appear under My NFTs.
- **Use Legacy Vault.** Name your beneficiaries and their shares, deposit FLR, FXRP or USDT into the Legacy Vault smart contract, and check in on a schedule you choose. If a check-in is missed, each beneficiary can claim their share from the contract.

## What is coming next

Token swaps through SparkDEX V3 pools and FXRP staking through the Firelight stXRP vault are being prepared and are marked “launching soon” in the app. Borrowing against FXRP is planned. A 555 NFT collection is in development, and its waitlist is open. We label a feature as available only once it is live on mainnet.

## Principles

- **Self-custody.** Flare Vault never holds your keys. It cannot move your funds without you approving the transaction in your own wallet, and it never asks for a recovery phrase or private key.
- **Privacy first.** The app sees your public wallet address and balances, which anyone can already read on the blockchain explorer. Nothing else is required to use it.
- **Transparency.** Every contract Flare Vault uses is public and can be opened on the Flare explorer. Fees are shown before you confirm.
- **Simplicity.** Four steps for Legacy Vault: connect, set the plan, deposit, check in.

## Be clear about the trade-offs

Funds you keep in your own wallet stay in your wallet. Funds you choose to deposit into Legacy Vault are held by the Legacy Vault smart contract until you withdraw them or your beneficiaries claim them. That is what lets beneficiaries claim without you, and it means you rely on that contract’s code. No third-party security audit has been published yet. The Legacy Vault source code is verified and public on the Flare explorer, so anyone can read exactly what it does before depositing. The full technical detail is in the [documentation](https://www.flarevaultapp.xyz/docs).
